# Deployed-bytecode claim map: EIP-8282 builder requests

## 5.13 EIP-8282 builder requests: registration, exits and payments across the layers

This family's rows are stated on the joint execution–consensus chain at Gloas, in §5.13.1.

### 5.13.1 Across the layers: EIP-8282 on the joint execution–consensus chain (Gloas)

The rows range over a **valid Gloas joint chain**, `Blanc.Lift.BuilderRequest.ValidJointG` (`Blanc/Composition/BuilderRequestJointG.lean:121`). It is made of:
- a checkpoint at which both builder contracts have their canonical code and fresh storage and the system address has no code (noSystemCode), with no further condition on how the checkpoint was reached;
- a configured execution history from the checkpoint to the anchor's built-upon chain (preBlocks, prefixHistory);
- a Gloas joint run from the anchor, `Blanc.GJointRun` (`Blanc/JointGloasHistory.lean:291`), of steps of three kinds: a pre-Gloas Electra/Fulu step, the Fulu→Gloas crossing, and a Gloas step;
- the anchor data:
  - `Blanc.Lift.BuilderRequest.AnchorPendingTrace` (`Blanc/Composition/BuilderRequestJointG.lean:56`): a delivered anchor payload passed the envelope check;
  - `Blanc.Lift.BuilderRequest.GAnchorInv` (`Blanc/Composition/BuilderRequestJointG.lean:46`): builder pubkeys unique, pending entries reference builders in range, exited builders have nothing pending, pending plus exposure covered by the balance, the builder withdrawal index (next_withdrawal_builder_index) in range, no live weight on the latest bid, latest-bid coherence, the latest bid's builder active, future payment slots empty, and **FinalizedNotAhead** (the finalized epoch is not ahead of the current epoch; FinalizedNotAheadF at a pre-Gloas anchor). FinalizedNotAhead is an anchor invariant, preserved by every step;
  - `Blanc.Lift.BuilderRequest.AnchorBidWitness` (`Blanc/Composition/BuilderRequestJointG.lean:101`): each pending entry and the latest bid is backed by a signed bid that verifies under real BLS, injectively;
  - `Blanc.GJointInv` (`Blanc/JointGloasInvariants.lean:85`), carried by every step;
- no transaction sender, EIP-7702 authority or CREATE frame at SYSTEM_ADDRESS over the whole history; the deposit-value budget (at most 2^256) and at most 2^64 exit submissions;
- the preset and configuration bounds: 2·SLOTS_PER_EPOCH ≤ EFFECTIVE_BALANCE_INCREMENT, 1 ≤ MIN_ATTESTATION_INCLUSION_DELAY, 0 < MIN_BUILDER_WITHDRAWABILITY_DELAY < 2^63, 2 ≤ SLOTS_PER_EPOCH;
- the two registry bounds, a named premise: every joint state of the run has at most 2^40 validators and at most 2^40 builders. They exclude chains that register or onboard more than 2^40 of either;
- B2's value-clause premises (`roots`, depositNoSender, exitNoSender, creditsFit, `codes`), read by `ValidJointG.deposit_value` (the B2 value-clause row).

**Execution side is the built-upon chain.** A payload counts only if a later block builds on it FULL. A payload delivered and then settled EMPTY is orphaned: its transactions, the deposit payment included, are not on the history the rows speak about.

**Scope of every row:** along any valid Gloas joint chain from an admissible anchor, under Jaune's Gloas consensus model at consensus-specs v1.7.0-beta.4 and Jaune's Amsterdam execution rules, about the mainnet EIP-8282 contracts at `0x0000bFF46984e3725691FA540a8C7589300D8282` (deposit) and `0x000064D678505ad48F8cCb093BC65613800E8282` (exit).

**BLS.** Every row is quantified over Jaune's BLS switch **except B1's main theorem and B5(ii)**, which add `bls = true`. With the switch off, signature checks are stubbed true, so the other rows do not include BLS validity. This includes the signature clause of the B1 fork theorem: `crossing_onboards` holds for either setting, and "valid DOMAIN_DEPOSIT signature" means Jaune's real BLS only at `bls = true`.

**Outcomes are read at mid-list states.** Each request is classified at the state the specs process it at (after the parent settlement and the earlier requests of the same list). The trace is read at the step's post-slots state (`D.postSlots`), the state Gloas process_block receives. A block whose uint64 arithmetic would overflow is invalid, so it is not a step.

**Citations.** The Theorem column cites Blanc declarations at this commit. The Jaune-side evidence column names declarations of Jaune at the pinned revision, with their file and line there; Blanc's checks do not resolve that column.

## Disclosures and limits

63. **List versus hash_tree_root.** The specs tie a child's parent_execution_requests to its parent's payload only by hash_tree_root, and the envelope's withdrawals to payload_expected_withdrawals only by hash_tree_root. The joint step compares the values: the child's requests must equal the delivered envelope's requests (all five lists), the requests the execution engine produced must equal the envelope's list, the payload's withdrawals must equal payload_expected_withdrawals as lists, and a child of a parent whose payload is already the head must carry empty requests by value. It also runs the specs' own root checks, so it accepts fewer chains than the specs. A chain the specs accept and the joint step refuses can exist only through a collision of the SSZ hash_tree_root hash (SHA-256). Every positional claim in B1 to B4 and B6 rests on this. **The roots are Gloas-orientation (right-spine) progressive roots:** at beta.4 EIP-7688 progressive lists hash `(subtree, successor)`, where v1.6.1 hashes `(successor, subtree)`. Serialization and the active-fields word agree. The collision statement is about that Gloas hash.

64. **BLS.** B1's main theorem and B5(ii) are stated with Jaune's real BLS (`bls = true`). Every other row holds for either setting of the switch; with the switch off, signature checks are stubbed true and those rows do not include BLS validity. blsVerify is Jaune's Bool predicate with key-validation and subgroup checks; the claim is about it, not about unforgeability.

65. **EIP-8282 text versus the pinned spec on top-ups.** The EIP's later text says a known pubkey's withdrawal_credentials and `signature` are ignored on a deposit. The pinned consensus text checks the `0xB0` prefix **before** the known/new split, so a top-up with non-builder credentials is ignored and its stake is forfeited (stays with the contract). The pinned text governs B2 and B1. At the fork, upgrade_to_gloas's in-loop top-ups of an already-registered builder do not check credentials or signature (the `T` class of OnboardingSpec).

66. **Signature authorization (B1).** B1 says a valid proof of possession existed for the registered key and credentials. It does not say who paid for the submission, and a signed record can be replayed by anyone (no nonce). The execution_address is whatever the signed credentials name.

67. **Orphaned payloads have no B2 fate.** The quantifier is the built-upon history. A payload delivered and then settled EMPTY is dropped; its transactions, a deposit payment included, are not on that history, so no theorem assigns that payment a consensus fate.

68. **"A slashed proposer's payment is removed" needs the old-parent qualifier.** In B5(iii), clearedBySlash holds only when no direct append follows. If the child that settles the parent arrives two or more epochs after the bid, the old-parent branch appends the bid's withdrawal from the bid itself, even though the payment entry was cleared. The builder is charged anyway.

69. **Payout liveness is a stated non-claim.** B5 says where balance goes, not that it ever arrives. The builders' pass (get_builders_sweep_withdrawals) competes with the pending-withdrawals prefix, the partial-withdrawals loop and the validators' pass (get_validators_sweep_withdrawals) for the payload's withdrawal slots, advances a wrapping pointer, and is skipped on every EMPTY-parent block.

70. **The checkpoint premise.** `ValidJointG` asks only that the system address has no code at the checkpoint, plus the builder contracts' code and fresh storage there. No other condition on how the checkpoint was reached.

71. **Anchor invariants.** No row takes a hypothesis beyond those it states. FinalizedNotAhead is an anchor invariant (not a canonical-chain, timing or honest-majority premise); the positive builder withdrawability delay and the auxiliary invariants LatestBidBuilderActive and FutureEmpty are likewise anchor premises; that the built-upon chain's head hash is the beacon state's latest block hash is a premise at a pre-Gloas anchor and is satisfiable there.

72. **The registry bounds and clearedAtAnchor.** B5(ii) and B7 assume at most 2^40 validators and 2^40 builders at every state of the run, a named premise. Without them a builder at index `2^40 + k` is debited as builder `k`, and both rows are false. clearedAtAnchor means that the latest bid's payment is not live at the anchor (an empty entry, or its slot already left the two-epoch payments window), and no direct append follows.

73. **B5(ii)'s signer tie and its limits.** B5(ii) states that, for a debit that moves value, the bid was signed under the pubkey of the builder at the debited entry's index, at the state process_withdrawals reads. Three limits:
    - **Positive amounts only.** The tie holds only for a debit with `0 < amount`. The anchor invariant that exited builders have nothing pending counts amounts, so a zero-amount pending anchor withdrawal on an exited builder is allowed, and that builder's slot can be reused; such a zero debit carries no signer tie.
    - **The index link holds below 2^40.** The clause states `bi.toNat < 2^40 → bi = e.builder_index`, because `Routes bi w` (the routing filter) does not fix the routed index `bi` above 2^40. With the registry bounds, every builder index is below 2^40.
    - **The bid's pre-state is existential.** `BidChargedBy` names the signing key as the pubkey of the bid's builder at a state `s3` from which process_execution_payload_bid succeeds; `s3` is existential, exactly as in `BidCharged`.

74. **B5(iv)'s EL credit is `mod 2^256`, on the actual kept state.** The credit is stated on the actual post-transaction state of the kept block of the first built-upon payload (`KeptStep cfg b D'.postExec`, and the block's request pass starts from the withdrawals folded over that state), so it is tied to the kept chain. It stays `mod 2^256`: that is the EVM's balance semantics, and there is no no-wrap bound for Gloas steps.

75. **The count semantics are fates, not pushes.** "At most once" counts fate predicates. An anchor withdrawal entry counts 0, so `anchor_witness_charged_once` means "appended", not "paid". The anchor's pending withdrawal entries are not counted at all: `witnessAppends` has a summand for them, but each of its terms is identically 0 (`anchorAppends_withdrawal`), and the proof does not use witness injectivity against them. So `anchor_witness_charged_once` bounds the run's appends of the payments entries and the latest bid only; it does not state that a witness bid already queued in the anchor's builder_pending_withdrawals is not also appended during the run.

76. **The controls are function-level.** They are closed-state mutants and witnesses on named functions. The B6 control (i) shows only that the mutant is distinguishable. The B5 mutant, the B1 fork controls and the B5(ii) signer control (`g304_signer_control`) exist. The B2 `bls = true` witness w_register_blsOn takes a signature-verifies hypothesis that is satisfiable in principle; no closed instance carries a real key pair, and the `bls = false` witness carries the case. The conservation control is against a local total. No witnesses exist for the anchor fates.

77. **The B2 value clause.** `ValidJointG.deposit_value` states the deposit-contract clause and, in addition, the same "final balance at least checkpoint balance plus what the kept frames paid" for the exit contract. The value comparison uses amounts taken from lists the joint step has equated by value; the specs equate them by root. The caveat of item 63 applies.

78. **B6 is silent on short runs.** The latency bound holds only for payloads that exist; on a run too short to reach the payload the theorem says nothing.

79. **The B1 fork theorem is composed from parts.** There is no single run-level theorem for "every builder record created after the fork": it is `registration_authorization` for Gloas steps plus `crossing_onboards`, `crossing_builders` and `crossing_onboarding` for the crossing (the Fulu→Gloas step). Its signature clause is real BLS only at `bls = true`.

80. **B4's unique resolution is stated over initiated exits.** `exit_resolution_unique` speaks of exits initiated at a step (B3's ExitInitiated form). There is no named lemma bridging it to the `initiate` outcome in `exit_fate`; an `initiate` at the mid-list state is an initiated exit by reading, not by a cited theorem.
